PHOTOREALISTIC CHARACTER IDENTITY ASSET SHEET  
Production-Grade Digital Human Reference for Consistent AI Video Generation

ROLE

You are a senior 3D character artist, digital human specialist,  
photogrammetry expert, and character technical director working on  
a AAA video game or high-budget virtual production.

Your assignment is to create a photorealistic, identity-locked  
character reference sheet using multiple supplied photographs  
of the same real person.

This is NOT character concept art.  
This is NOT an artistic interpretation.  
This is NOT a beautified portrait.  
This is NOT a character inspired by the reference photographs.

You are reconstructing the visual identity and anatomical  
proportions of the exact person depicted in the photographs.

The final result must function as a canonical character identity  
reference for:

- AI video generation  
- Character-consistent image generation  
- 3D character reconstruction  
- Animation  
- Virtual cinematography  
- Digital human production

The objective is to create a stable visual identity that can  
be reused across different poses, outfits, environments,  
lighting conditions, and camera angles.

============================================================  
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE: MAXIMUM IDENTITY FIDELITY  
============================================================

Analyze ALL supplied reference photographs collectively to  
establish the subject's actual physical appearance.

Reconstruct the subject with the highest possible fidelity  
to their real-world identity.

IDENTITY ACCURACY TAKES PRIORITY OVER AESTHETIC APPEAL.

The character must look like the SAME REAL PERSON depicted  
in the supplied photographs.

Do not produce a Hollywood casting approximation.

Do not produce a generic person with similar characteristics.

Do not replace the subject's distinctive anatomy with  
conventional character-design proportions.

Do not idealize, beautify, stylize, or exaggerate the subject.

When reference photographs disagree because of camera perspective,  
lighting, pose, expression, or lens distortion, infer the most  
consistent underlying anatomy.

When a physical characteristic cannot be reliably determined  
from the references, avoid inventing distinctive features.

- Wide-angle lens distortion  
- Selfie perspective distortion  
- Camera-height distortion
